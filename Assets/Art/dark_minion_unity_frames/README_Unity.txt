Dark Minion - Unity-ready individual PNG frames
================================================

All sprites are PNG with transparent background.
Every frame uses the same canvas size: 240x192px.
Pixel art was kept at the original scale; no resizing/filtering was applied.

Animation frame counts:
- idle: 4 frames
- walk_down: 9 frames
- walk_left: 9 frames
- walk_right: 9 frames
- walk_up: 6 frames
- attack: 8 frames
- hurt: 6 frames
- die: 9 frames

Suggested Unity import:
- Texture Type: Sprite (2D and UI)
- Sprite Mode: Single
- Filter Mode: Point (no filter)
- Compression: None
- Use the same Pixels Per Unit for every frame
